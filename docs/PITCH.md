# Tijah - Product Pitch

## The Problem

Tens of millions of informal traders in Nigeria run their businesses with zero digital records. They track sales in their heads, lose money to forgotten debts, and can't tell if they're profitable. Existing tools (POS apps, accounting software) don't work for them — they require downloads, literacy, and formal business concepts.

## The Solution

**Tijah is a WhatsApp shop assistant.** Traders send a voice note — "I sold 3 bags of rice for 5 thousand each" — and Tijah records it. No app to download. No sign-up. No typing required.

Tijah handles:
- **Sales** — record what you sold, automatically track revenue
- **Stock** — know what you have before it runs out
- **Credit book** — who owes you, how much, shareable receipt when they deny it
- **Expenses** — track where your money goes
- **Summary** — "How did my shop do today?" gives you the answer

Everything works by voice note in English or Nigerian Pidgin.

## Why It Works

- **WhatsApp is already there.** 98% of Nigerian smartphone users have it. Zero friction.
- **Voice-first.** Designed for people who prefer talking to typing.
- **Bilingual.** Understands English and Pidgin, switches automatically.
- **No dead ends.** Always responds, always helpful, never crashes silently.

## Traction

- Alpha deployed and live on WhatsApp
- Voice transcription (Whisper) + AI understanding (Gemini Flash) working end-to-end
- Shareable web reports for each shop
- Testing with real traders in progress

## How It Makes Money (Future)

1. **Freemium:** Free for basic use, paid tier for advanced reports, alerts, and multi-staff
2. **Financial services:** Credit scoring data for microfinance partners (with consent)
3. **Supplier marketplace:** Connect traders to wholesale suppliers
