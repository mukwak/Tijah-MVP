# Tijah User Testing Guide

**What is Tijah?** A WhatsApp shop assistant for recording sales, stock, credit, and expenses — by voice or text.

**Test number:** +_____________ (save to contacts, then send "hi" on WhatsApp)

---

## Test Tasks

Try each task below. After each one, note: did it work? Was anything confusing?

### 0. Send a voice greeting
- Press the mic button and say "hello" or "how far" as a voice note
- Check: did Tijah reply with both text and a voice note?

### 1. Set up your shop
- Send "hi" as a text message and follow the welcome message
- When asked, give your shop a name (e.g. "My shop is called Blessing Store")

### 2. Record a sale
- **Text:** "I sold 3 bags of rice for 5000 each"
- **Voice:** press the mic and say the same thing naturally

### 3. Buy stock
- "I bought 10 bags of cement at 3000"

### 4. Record a credit sale (customer owes you)
- "Mama Joy bought 2 bags on credit, 5000 each"
- Then: "Mama Joy paid 3000"
- Then: "Who owes me?"

### 5. Record an expense
- "I spent 500 on transport"

### 6. Check your shop
- "How my shop do today?" or "What did I sell today?"
- "How much rice do I have?"

### 7. Fix a mistake
- "Cancel that" or "Undo"

### 8. Get your report
- Say "my report" — Tijah sends a link. Open it in your browser.

### 9. Get a customer receipt
- "Receipt for Mama Joy" — Tijah sends a link you can share with the customer.
- Open it: does it show only Mama Joy's records (not your whole shop)?

## Things to Know

- **First reply may be slow** (up to 1 min if Tijah was idle). After that it's fast.
- Voice in = voice reply. Text in = text reply.
- You can speak English or Pidgin — Tijah understands both.
- Say "I have a complaint" anytime something goes wrong.
