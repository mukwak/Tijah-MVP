# Tijah Alpha Tester Guide

Thank you for testing Tijah! Tijah is your shop assistant on WhatsApp. You talk to it like a person - voice note or text - and it keeps your records: sales, stock, money people owe you, and your expenses.

## How to start

1. Save the Tijah number as a contact: **+_____________** (the alpha test number)
2. Send it any message on WhatsApp, like "hi"
3. That's it. No app to download, no sign-up.

## What you can say

Talk normally, in English or Pidgin. Voice notes work - just press the mic and talk.

**Selling:**
- "I sold 3 bags of rice for 5 thousand each"
- "I sell 2 crate of minerals, 4,500"

**Buying stock:**
- "I bought 10 bags of cement at 3000"

**Credit (people owing you):**
- "Mama Joy owes me 5 thousand"
- "Mama Joy don pay 2000"
- "Who owes me money?"

**Expenses:**
- "I spent 500 on transport"

**Checking your shop:**
- "How did my shop do today?"
- "How much rice do I have?"
- "What did I sell this week?"

**Fixing mistakes:**
- "Cancel that" / "Undo"
- "It was 3 bags, not 5"

**Your full records:**
- Say "my report" and Tijah sends you a private link showing everything - all your sales, stock, credits and expenses. Open it in your browser anytime. It updates automatically.

**Customer receipt (proof of debt):**
- "Receipt for Mama Joy" - Tijah sends a link showing only what that customer owes and has paid. You can share this link with the customer.

**Language:**
- "Speak pidgin" / "Speak english"

## Things to know during the alpha

- **First reply can be slow.** If Tijah has been quiet for a while, the first message may take up to a minute. After that it's fast.
- **Voice replies:** if you send a voice note, Tijah replies with a voice note too.
- **Your report link is private.** Anyone with the link can see your shop records, so only share it with people you trust.
